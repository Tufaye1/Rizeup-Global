Studying abroad doesn't have to be expensive. With the right country and a scholarship, your total cost can be lower than a private university at home. Here are the most affordable options for Bangladeshi students — and how to study for free (or close to it).

## The cheapest countries to study abroad

- **Malaysia** — the most affordable overall for Bangladeshi students. Tuition **from around $3,500/yr** and living costs close to Dhaka.
- **Germany** — **free or near-free public tuition**; you mainly budget for living costs.
- **Italy** — low public tuition plus strong regional scholarships that can cover living costs.
- **Other low-cost European countries** — several offer affordable, English-taught degrees.

The USA and Australia are pricier on paper, but scholarships can make specific universities surprisingly affordable — so don't rule them out before checking the *net* cost.

## How to study abroad for free (realistically)

"Free" usually means one of two things:

1. **Free/low tuition + affordable living** — e.g. a public university in Germany where you only cover living costs.
2. **A strong scholarship at an affordable university** — e.g. a 40–100% tuition waiver in Malaysia, so your remaining cost is small.

A genuine 100% free ride (tuition + living, everything) is rare and highly competitive. The smart strategy is combining a low-cost country with a scholarship you actually qualify for.

## What really drives your total cost

- **Tuition** — varies hugely; often the smaller number in low-cost countries.
- **Living costs** — rent, food and transport; this is where cities differ most.
- **One-off costs** — visa, flights, insurance, and (for some countries) a blocked account.

A counsellor can map your *real* total — not just the tuition sticker price — against your family budget.

## Cutting your cost further

- Choose an **affordable city**, not just an affordable country.
- Apply for **merit scholarships** and MOI-friendly universities to skip test costs.
- Consider **part-time work** rights (available in many destinations).
- Start early to catch the best-funded scholarship rounds.
